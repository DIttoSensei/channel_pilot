Questions, answered

A few things
you should know.

Want to understand the setup before you start? Here are the common questions.

Do I need video editing experience?

No. The workflow handles the production steps, and you stay in control of the category and the final result.

What kind of videos does Channel Pilot make?

Narration-led, faceless videos in vertical 9:16 format, across twelve categories including mystery, horror, sci-fi, history and educational. Each category has its own writing style, voices and visuals.

How long does a video take?

Most videos are ready in about 5 to 15 minutes. You don’t need to keep the page open. Come back later and your video will be waiting in your dashboard, where it’s kept for 24 hours.

Do I need my own accounts or API keys?

You connect a free Cloudflare account in one click inside the generator. It’s used to create the images for your videos. We handle everything else.

How does billing work?

You choose 2 weeks, 1 month, 3 months or 1 year and pay once. There is no automatic renewal, so nothing to cancel. After payment we email you a license key, and your time starts when you activate it in the generator. When it ends, buy a new key. If you renew early, your remaining days carry over.

Does Channel Pilot publish to YouTube automatically?

No. It creates the finished video file for you to review and download. You decide when and where to publish it.

Will this guarantee views or monetization?

No. Channel Pilot helps with video production. It can’t guarantee views, subscribers, revenue or YouTube monetization. Channel strategy and audience response still matter.
